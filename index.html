CIVIC SCIENCE & LAST-MILE WARNING

Intelligence that reaches the last mile

The North Eastern Region experiences some of the highest rainfall gradients in the world. Our platform unifies IMD Doppler precipitation grids, automated in-situ soil moisture piezometers, Sentinel-1 InSAR surface deformation, 40-year historical landslide atlases, and verified citizen reports into one live predictive engine.

01

Detect

In-situ geotechnical sensors, IMD Doppler radars, and Sentinel-1 InSAR satellites monitor ground saturation and millimeter slope creep.

02

Predict

Multivariate physics-informed AI algorithms cross-reference 40-year geological models with live rainfall thresholds to forecast collapse probability.

03

Alert

Automated Common Alerting Protocol (CAP) relays localized siren triggers, SMS broadcasts, and district emergency dispatches within seconds.
